Who we are

Kingdom Crew Fellowship

Kingdom Crew Fellowship is a Christian student fellowship at UNILAK, Rwamagana Campus, Rwanda. We gather every Sunday to pray, worship, study the Bible and encourage one another.

To help students at UNILAK grow in their relationship with Jesus Christ through prayer, worship, Bible study and loving community, and to serve others with Christ's love.
